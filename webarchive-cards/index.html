How It Works

Get started in three simple steps. No account required.

1

Install Extension

Add WebArchive Cards to Chrome from the Chrome Web Store. One click installation.

2

Save Pages

Click the extension icon or press ⌘+Shift+S on any web page to save it as a card.

3

Find Anything

Open the side panel to browse, search, and filter your saved cards instantly.
